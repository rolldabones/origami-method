For Stage 6, the gates. G1 input validity, auto at intake: pass if the source is published notes carrying the meeting date, the attendee list, numbered agenda headings and the publication date, and the roster is present; fail routing by cause: notes lacking the meeting date, the attendee list, numbered agenda headings or the publication date go back to the publisher naming the missing element, and the clock pauses; a missing roster goes back to the support office, which supplies it; a forbidden source is rejected to the requester citing the rule; in every failure nothing passes to the Producer. No previous action list (the first meeting under this workflow) is not a failure: the list passes and the Carried over section reads "None: no previous list". G2 schema, auto at self-check: the output matches the skeleton section by section; fail, the Producer redrafts. G3 traceability, auto at self-check: every row carries an agenda item and a quoted trigger phrase; fail, the row moves to Unknowns. G4 roster match, auto at self-check: every owner is explicitly assigned and on the roster; fail, the owner is set to Unknown and the row flagged and kept. G5 no inference, Red-Team reported to the Clerk: each row's trigger phrase is an assignment, not a suggestion; row-level fail, the row moves to Possible actions; list-level fail, the list returns to draft. G6 redaction, auto at self-check: no private individual's name outside the [private individual] marker; fail, the name is replaced by the marker and the row noted. G7 Chair consultation, human, before Clerk sign-off: every flag the Clerk confirmed has a recorded Chair (or Vice-Chair) view, and every flag the Clerk cleared carries the Clerk's recorded reason; fail, the row is held from circulation until the view or the reason is recorded. G8 timing, auto: the list reaches the Clerk within two town working days of publication; at risk on day one, a notice goes to the Clerk, who decides whether to extend; missed, when the list has not reached the Clerk by the end of the second working day or of an extension the Clerk granted, the Clerk is told at once, nothing is circulated until G9 passes, and the miss is entered in the Workflow Record with its cause. G9 Clerk sign-off, human: the Clerk approves the list for circulation; fail, the list returns to draft with comments. G10 Chair flag, auto at self-check: every row and every possible action whose text contains a keyword is marked Chair Y; fail, the Producer sets the flag and notes it.

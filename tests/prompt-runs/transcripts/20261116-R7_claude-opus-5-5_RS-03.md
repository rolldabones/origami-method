# Stage 3: Assign Risk Level

## What this stage does

At this stage we decide how much could go wrong and who answers for it. There are four parts:

1. **Impact:** how much harm a wrong output could cause if it reached members.
2. **Likelihood:** how often the workflow is likely to produce a wrong output without controls.
3. **Overall level:** calculated from Impact and Likelihood using a fixed rule, not judgment.
4. **RACI:** who does the work (Responsible), who answers for the result (Accountable), who is asked before decisions (Consulted), and who is told afterwards (Informed). We also set **mitigations** (controls that reduce risk) and **escalation triggers** (conditions that stop the run and pass it to a human).

**The Overall rule:**

| Impact \ Likelihood | Low | Med | High |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Low** | Low | Low | Med |
| **Med** | Low | Med | High |
| **High** | Med | High | High |

There is also a floor: if Stage 3.5 classifies the data as Confidential or Restricted, Overall cannot be lower than Med. Your inputs are described as public, but the sensitivity class is yours to confirm at Stage 3.5. Until then, Overall is **provisional**.

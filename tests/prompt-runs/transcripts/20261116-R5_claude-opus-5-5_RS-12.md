**How the method works**

We design the workflow, not the action list. My role is to help you build a repeatable, safe process that a person or AI can later run. I will not draft action lists or sample outputs.

We work in fixed stages, from 0 to 9. At each stage I summarize, give you a template or checklist, and ask you to confirm before we continue. Missing information is marked **"Unknown / Insufficient data"**, and we do not skip stages.

The method uses a folding metaphor:

| Term | Plain meaning |
|---|---|
| **Sheet** | Project materials: goal, inputs, people, limits |
| **Creases** | Input/output rules: what the notes must look like coming in, and the skeleton the action list must follow going out |
| **Folds** | Small, planned, logged changes to the workflow |
| **Unfolding** | Running test cases, inspecting results, correcting |
| **Lock** | Making a proven step mandatory |
| **Expose** | Adding checks that reveal where something fails |

- the approver's name
- the artifact and its version
- the date
- the word "approved"

A chat confirmation moves us to the next stage, but it is not an approval.

Once an artifact is approved, any change to it is logged as a fold. The change reopens that stage and voids every later approval until we re-test and re-approve.

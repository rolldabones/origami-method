## Stage 0: Orientation

**The method.** We build the workflow like folding paper: deliberately, one step at a time, with every change recorded.

| Term | Plain meaning |
|---|---|
| **Sheet** | Project materials: goal, inputs, people, limits |
| **Creases** | Input/output rules: how inputs must look and the required output structure |
| **Folds** | Planned, minimal changes to the workflow |
| **Unfolding** | Testing and fixing: run cases, inspect, correct |
| **Lock** | Making a proven step mandatory |
| **Expose** | Adding diagnostics so failures are visible |

**How each stage runs:**
1. I summarize where we are.
2. I present the stage's artifact as a template or checklist.
3. You confirm before we continue.

**Ground rules:**
- Missing information is marked "Unknown / Insufficient data" until you supply it.
- We don't skip stages.
- I design the workflow. I don't produce its deliverables (notes, invoice reviews).
- Formal approvals (Risk & RACI, Data Handling, Design, Release) require a named approver, the artifact and version, a date and the word "approved." A chat "yes" moves us forward but isn't an approval of record.

> *"We are not trying to be clever. We are trying to be repeatable and safe."*

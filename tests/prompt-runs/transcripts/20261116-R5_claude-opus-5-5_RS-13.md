## How the method works

We build the workflow the way you'd fold a sheet of paper: deliberately, one crease at a time, testing as we go.

| Term | Plain meaning | What it means for your workflow |
|---|---|---|
| **Sheet** | Project materials | Your goal, the committee notes, the people involved, your limits |
| **Creases** | Input/output rules | What acceptable notes look like; the exact shape of the action list |
| **Folds** | Controlled change | Small, logged edits after testing |
| **Unfolding** | Test and fix | Running sample notes through and inspecting results |
| **Lock** | Freeze | Making a proven step mandatory |
| **Expose** | Reveal | Adding checks that show where things fail |

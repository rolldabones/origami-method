## How the Origami Method Works

We design **one AI workflow** by moving through Stages 0–9 in order. At each stage I will:

1. **Summarize** what we have.
2. **Present an artifact** (template or checklist).
3. **Ask for your confirmation** before we continue.

We don't skip stages, and missing information is marked **"Unknown / Insufficient data"** rather than guessed. My role is to help you design the workflow. I will not produce the action list itself or sample entries from it.

**Vocabulary (metaphor → plain language):**

| Term | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **Sheet** | Project materials: goal, inputs, people, limits |
| **Creases** | Input/output rules: what inputs must look like and what the output skeleton is |
| **Folds** | Controlled change: planned, minimal edits to the workflow |
| **Unfolding** | Testing and fixing: running cases, inspecting results, correcting problems |

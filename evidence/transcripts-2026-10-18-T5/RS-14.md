## How the Origami Method works

| Metaphor | Plain meaning |
|---|---|
| **Sheet** | Your project materials: goal, inputs, people, limits |
| **Creases** | Input/output rules: what goes in, what shape comes out |
| **Folds** | Small, planned changes to the workflow, each logged |
| **Unfolding** | Test and fix: run cases, inspect, correct |
| **Lock** | Make a step mandatory once it is proven |
| **Expose** | Add diagnostics so failures are visible |

We go through one stage at a time, from 0 to 9. At each stage I summarize, give you a template, and ask you to confirm before we move on. If information is missing, I mark it **"Unknown / Insufficient data"** rather than guess.

Meeting notes often contain names, opinions, decisions and sometimes confidential or personal data. That matters for Stage 3 (Risk) and Stage 3.5 (Data Handling), so precise answers now will save rework later.

## How the Origami Method works

We treat your workflow like a sheet of paper that gets folded into a reliable shape:

| Term | Plain meaning | Where it happens |
|---|---|---|
| **Sheet** | Project materials: goal, inputs, people, limits | Stages 1–3.5 |
| **Creases** | Input/output rules: what inputs must look like and what the output skeleton is | Stage 4 |
| **Folds** | Controlled, minimal, logged changes to the workflow | Stage 9 |
| **Unfolding** | Testing: run cases, inspect, fix | Stage 8 |
| **Lock** | Freeze a proven step so it becomes mandatory | Stage 9 |
| **Expose** | Add diagnostics so failures become visible | Stage 9 |

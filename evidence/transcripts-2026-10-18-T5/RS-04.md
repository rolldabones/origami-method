## How the method works

We design the workflow, not its output. I will not produce the action list or any sample of it. My job is to help you build a process that produces it reliably and safely.

**The metaphor, in plain language:**

- **Sheet** (project materials) is your goal, inputs, people and limits. Stages 1–3.5 lay this out.
- **Creases** (input/output rules) define how the committee notes must arrive and what the action list must look like. That is Stage 4.
- **Folds** (controlled change) are small, planned edits to the workflow, each logged with a reason. That is Stage 9.
- **Unfolding** (test and fix) means running test cases, inspecting the results and correcting faults. That is Stage 8.
- **Lock** (freeze) makes a step mandatory once it has proven itself.
- **Expose** (reveal) adds checkpoints so that failures become visible instead of silent.

**Rules we will follow:**

- We cover one stage at a time. At each stage I summarize, present a template, and ask you to confirm before we continue.
- If information is missing, I mark it **"Unknown / Insufficient data"** and ask you for it.
- We do not skip stages. If you ask to skip one, I will explain the risk and return to the next valid stage.

- Builder Packets for the custom GPTs are issued only after Stage 7.5 passes, and only as **Draft**. They become **Released** only after every test case passes and the decision owner records the release approval at 8C.
